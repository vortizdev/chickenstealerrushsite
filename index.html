Steal their chickens. Guard your own.

A fast 1v1 card battle for Android. Send your goblins to raid the coop and your rival's nests, and stop their stealers before they get home. Every match lasts 2 minutes 30 seconds.

How a match plays

Trailer coming soon

  • Steal

    Spend Grub to send a goblin after chickens. Sneaky Swipe, Chicken Glider and Tunnel Dig each go a different way: tiptoeing, gliding or digging.

  • Guard

    Read the thief and play the right trap before it reaches your nest. Get it wrong and the chicken is gone.

  • Win

    Fill all 9 nest slots, or hold the most chickens when the clock hits zero. A tie goes to Sudden Death: the next delivery wins.

Chicken Stealer Rush is a real-time card battle where two players each command three goblins guarding three nests. Players spend Grub to send goblins raiding the shared coop or each other's nests, and play Nets, Spike Moats and Firebombs to defend and counter. Matches last 2 minutes 30 seconds.
