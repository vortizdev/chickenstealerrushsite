How a match plays

Steal

Sneaky Swipe, Chicken Glider and Tunnel Dig each send a goblin after chickens a different way: tiptoeing, gliding or digging.

Guard

Net catches Air and Sneak stealers. Spike Moat stops Ground and Rocket. Pick the right one before the thief arrives.

Win

Fill all 9 nest slots, or hold the most chickens when the clock hits zero. A tie goes to Sudden Death: the next delivery wins.

Ways to play

  • Live 1v1Real players, matched by Golden Eggs, your rank.
  • EndlessBeat bot after bot on Easy, Normal or Hard, and climb the leaderboards.
  • ClansUnlock the Pyro and Tinkers clans, their goblins and their cards along the Golden Egg Road.
  • PracticeTry a new deck against a bot anytime.

Chicken Stealer Rush is a real-time card battle where two players each command three goblins guarding three nests. Players spend Grub to send goblins raiding the shared coop or each other's nests, and play Nets, Spike Moats and Firebombs to defend and counter. Matches last 2 minutes 30 seconds.
